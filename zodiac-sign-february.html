Zodiac signs · February

What is the zodiac sign for February?

February has two zodiac signs: Aquarius until 18 February, and Pisces from 19 February.

Born on 18 or 19 February?

These are the usual calendar dates. The moment the Sun moves from Aquarius into Pisces shifts slightly between years, so a birthday on the boundary can fall either side. A birth chart calculated for the exact minute and place shows where the Sun stood.

Aquarius: the constellation Aquarius

Aquarius covers 20 January – 18 February. Its constellation is Aquarius, and its brightest star is Sadalsuud.

From the UK, Aquarius is best placed in the evening sky from September to November.

Pisces: the constellation Pisces

Pisces covers 19 February – 20 March. Its constellation is Pisces, and its brightest star is Alpherg.

From the UK, Pisces is best placed in the evening sky from October to December.
