Questions

How many zodiac signs are there?

Twelve, each covering about a month of the calendar.

Why does every month have two signs?

Each sign begins between the 19th and the 23rd of a month, so every month is shared by the sign that is ending and the one that is beginning.

What is the brightest star of each sign's constellation?

Hamal (Aries), Aldebaran (Taurus), Pollux (Gemini), Tarf (Cancer), Regulus (Leo), Spica (Virgo), Zubeneschamali (Libra), Antares (Scorpius), Kaus Australis (Sagittarius), Deneb Algedi (Capricornus), Sadalsuud (Aquarius) and Alpherg (Pisces).
