Zodiac signs · October

What is the zodiac sign for October?

October has two zodiac signs: Libra until 22 October, and Scorpio from 23 October.

Born on 22 or 23 October?

These are the usual calendar dates. The moment the Sun moves from Libra into Scorpio shifts slightly between years, so a birthday on the boundary can fall either side. A birth chart calculated for the exact minute and place shows where the Sun stood.

Libra: the constellation Libra

Libra covers 23 September – 22 October. Its constellation is Libra, and its brightest star is Zubeneschamali.

From the UK, Libra is best placed in the evening sky from May to July, low in the south.

Scorpio: the constellation Scorpius

Scorpio covers 23 October – 21 November. Its constellation is Scorpius, and its brightest star is Antares.

From the UK, Scorpius is best placed in the evening sky in June and July, low on the southern horizon.

What zodiac sign is October?

What zodiac sign is 23 October?

In the usual calendar dates, 23 October is the first day of Scorpio. The exact moment the Sun moves into the next sign shifts slightly from year to year, so a birth on 22 or 23 October can fall either side.

Which constellation is Scorpio?

The constellation Scorpius, whose brightest star is Antares.
