Zodiac signs · December

What is the zodiac sign for December?

December has two zodiac signs: Sagittarius until 21 December, and Capricorn from 22 December.

Born on 21 or 22 December?

These are the usual calendar dates. The moment the Sun moves from Sagittarius into Capricorn shifts slightly between years, so a birthday on the boundary can fall either side. A birth chart calculated for the exact minute and place shows where the Sun stood.

Sagittarius: the constellation Sagittarius

Sagittarius covers 22 November – 21 December. Its constellation is Sagittarius, and its brightest star is Kaus Australis.

From the UK, Sagittarius is best placed in the evening sky in July and August, very low in the south.

Capricorn: the constellation Capricornus

Capricorn covers 22 December – 19 January. Its constellation is Capricornus, and its brightest star is Deneb Algedi.

From the UK, Capricornus is best placed in the evening sky from August to October, low in the south.

Questions

What zodiac sign is December?

December has two zodiac signs: Sagittarius until 21 December and Capricorn from 22 December.

What zodiac sign is 22 December?

In the usual calendar dates, 22 December is the first day of Capricorn. The exact moment the Sun moves into the next sign shifts slightly from year to year, so a birth on 21 or 22 December can fall either side.

Which constellation is Capricorn?

The constellation Capricornus, whose brightest star is Deneb Algedi.
