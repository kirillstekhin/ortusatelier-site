Zodiac signs · July

What is the zodiac sign for July?

July has two zodiac signs: Cancer until 22 July, and Leo from 23 July.

Born on 22 or 23 July?

These are the usual calendar dates. The moment the Sun moves from Cancer into Leo shifts slightly between years, so a birthday on the boundary can fall either side. A birth chart calculated for the exact minute and place shows where the Sun stood.

Cancer: the constellation Cancer

Cancer covers 21 June – 22 July. Its constellation is Cancer, and its brightest star is Tarf.

From the UK, Cancer is best placed in the evening sky from February to April.

Leo: the constellation Leo

Leo covers 23 July – 22 August. Its constellation is Leo, and its brightest star is Regulus.

From the UK, Leo is best placed in the evening sky from March to May.
