About Us.

Ortus Atelier makes birth charts and family constellations, drawn on the real sky. Not a stock wheel with your details typed in — the actual positions of the Sun, the Moon and the planets, and the real stars behind them, calculated for your date, your minute and your birthplace.

2. How a Chart Is Made

Every order runs through our own astronomy code. It computes the Sun, the Moon and eight planets from an ephemeris checked against NASA JPL Horizons — the planets to within a tenth of a degree — finds the rising sign for the minute and place you give us, and draws the angles between the planets as petals. Behind the chart it places the real night sky over your birthplace: every star down to magnitude 6.4 that stood above the horizon. Family constellations are drawn the same way, from real star positions, with each constellation's brightest star named.

3. How It Is Printed

Each piece is printed to order in the UK as a giclée on 200 gsm fine-art paper, then shipped rolled or framed by hand in a gallery classic frame. Before anything is printed we email you the exact file, and orders are typically dispatched within 2–4 working days.

4. How You Pay

Payments are processed by Stripe. Card details are entered on Stripe's own secure checkout and are never seen or stored by us. Prices on this website are in pounds sterling and include VAT; UK delivery is free and already included in the price shown.
