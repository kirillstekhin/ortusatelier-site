Zodiac signs · November

What is the zodiac sign for November?

November has two zodiac signs: Scorpio until 21 November, and Sagittarius from 22 November.

Born on 21 or 22 November?

These are the usual calendar dates. The moment the Sun moves from Scorpio into Sagittarius shifts slightly between years, so a birthday on the boundary can fall either side. A birth chart calculated for the exact minute and place shows where the Sun stood.

Scorpio: the constellation Scorpius

Scorpio covers 23 October – 21 November. Its constellation is Scorpius, and its brightest star is Antares.

From the UK, Scorpius is best placed in the evening sky in June and July, low on the southern horizon.

Sagittarius: the constellation Sagittarius

Sagittarius covers 22 November – 21 December. Its constellation is Sagittarius, and its brightest star is Kaus Australis.

From the UK, Sagittarius is best placed in the evening sky in July and August, very low in the south.
