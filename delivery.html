Delivery & Returns.

Last updated: September 2026

Everything about how your chart is made, how it reaches you, and what happens if something isn’t right.

1. Free UK delivery

Standard delivery is free on every UK order — no minimum spend, no charge added at checkout. The price you see is the price you pay.

2. Made to order & dispatch times

Each chart is computed from astronomical data and printed to order in the UK — nothing is shipped from stock. Before printing, we email you a preview of the exact file and adjust anything you ask. Orders are typically dispatched within 2–4 working days, and a tracking number is emailed the moment your parcel leaves us. Delivery times after dispatch are carrier estimates and are not guaranteed — if your order hasn’t arrived within a reasonable time, email us and we’ll chase it.

3. Personalised goods — no change-of-mind returns

Every chart is personalised to the exact date, time, place and name you provide. Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, goods made to a consumer’s specification are exempt from the 14-day right to cancel. Once your order is in production we cannot accept a return or exchange because of a change of mind — so please check every detail on the preview.

4. Faulty, damaged or not as described

You are always fully protected where the fault is ours. If your print arrives damaged, faulty, misprinted, or different from the preview you approved, email admin@shopcienty.com within 30 days of delivery with a photo, and we will send a free replacement or a full refund — return postage on us where a return is needed.

5. A mistake in the details

If you spot an error in your date, time, place or spelling before the preview is approved, just reply to the preview email — corrections at that stage are free and unlimited. If a chart is printed with the details exactly as you supplied and approved them, a reprint with corrected details is chargeable, though we will always try to be kind about it.
