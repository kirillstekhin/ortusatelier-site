Zodiac signs · June

What is the zodiac sign for June?

June has two zodiac signs: Gemini until 20 June, and Cancer from 21 June.

Born on 20 or 21 June?

These are the usual calendar dates. The moment the Sun moves from Gemini into Cancer shifts slightly between years, so a birthday on the boundary can fall either side. A birth chart calculated for the exact minute and place shows where the Sun stood.

Gemini: the constellation Gemini

Gemini covers 21 May – 20 June. Its constellation is Gemini, and its brightest star is Pollux.

From the UK, Gemini is best placed in the evening sky from January to March.

Cancer: the constellation Cancer

Cancer covers 21 June – 22 July. Its constellation is Cancer, and its brightest star is Tarf.

From the UK, Cancer is best placed in the evening sky from February to April.
