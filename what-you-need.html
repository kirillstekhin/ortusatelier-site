Answers · Ordering

What do you need to order?

Family Constellations needs only first names and birthdays. Couple birth charts need two names, dates, birth times and birthplaces.

Family Constellations

First names and birthdays for two to six people. No birth times, no birthplaces.

Each person is shown as the constellation of their zodiac sign, drawn from real star positions and joined by a single thread. First names up to 24 characters, the family name up to 40. Three finishes: Midnight, Copper night and Paper.

Couple birth charts

Two names, two birth dates, two birth times and two birthplaces. Each chart is drawn for its own minute and place: the Sun, the Moon and eight planets to within a tenth of a degree, and the rising sign, which turns roughly a degree every four minutes — so give your best estimate of each time.

Four finishes: Copper bloom, Gold · Midnight, Garden on paper, Gold & silver.

For both

Prints from £34.99, classic frames from £69.99. Within 24 hours of your payment we email you the finished file to check. Made to order in your own country, dispatched within 3–6 working days, free tracked delivery to any UK or US address. There is no written interpretation on either print.

Questions

Do we need birth times for the family print?

No. Each person's constellation comes from their birthday alone.

Do we need birth times for the couple print?

Yes, one for each of you. If you don't know a time exactly, give your best estimate and watch the preview: the planets barely move in a few hours, but the rising sign does.

Is there a written interpretation?

No. The prints show where the stars and planets stood, drawn accurately, with nothing written about anyone.
