Zodiac signs · March

What is the zodiac sign for March?

March has two zodiac signs: Pisces until 20 March, and Aries from 21 March.

Born on 20 or 21 March?

These are the usual calendar dates. The moment the Sun moves from Pisces into Aries shifts slightly between years, so a birthday on the boundary can fall either side. A birth chart calculated for the exact minute and place shows where the Sun stood.

Pisces: the constellation Pisces

Pisces covers 19 February – 20 March. Its constellation is Pisces, and its brightest star is Alpherg.

From the UK, Pisces is best placed in the evening sky from October to December.

Aries: the constellation Aries

Aries covers 21 March – 19 April. Its constellation is Aries, and its brightest star is Hamal.

From the UK, Aries is best placed in the evening sky from November to January.
