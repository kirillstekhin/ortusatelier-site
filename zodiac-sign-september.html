Zodiac signs · September

What is the zodiac sign for September?

September has two zodiac signs: Virgo until 22 September, and Libra from 23 September.

Born on 22 or 23 September?

These are the usual calendar dates. The moment the Sun moves from Virgo into Libra shifts slightly between years, so a birthday on the boundary can fall either side. A birth chart calculated for the exact minute and place shows where the Sun stood.

Virgo: the constellation Virgo

Virgo covers 23 August – 22 September. Its constellation is Virgo, and its brightest star is Spica.

From the UK, Virgo is best placed in the evening sky from April to June.

Libra: the constellation Libra

Libra covers 23 September – 22 October. Its constellation is Libra, and its brightest star is Zubeneschamali.

From the UK, Libra is best placed in the evening sky from May to July, low in the south.

What zodiac sign is 23 September?

In the usual calendar dates, 23 September is the first day of Libra. The exact moment the Sun moves into the next sign shifts slightly from year to year, so a birth on 22 or 23 September can fall either side.

Which constellation is Libra?

The constellation Libra, whose brightest star is Zubeneschamali.
