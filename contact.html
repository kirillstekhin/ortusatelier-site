3. Before You Order

Every chart is drawn from the details you give us and printed to order in the UK or US. Before printing we email you the exact file; reply within 24 hours with any correction and we will fix it before anything goes to press. If you are unsure about a birth time, a place or a spelling, email us before ordering and we will check it with you.

Prices are shown in pounds sterling and include free UK and US delivery — see sizes & prices. Orders are typically dispatched within 2–4 working days.

4. A Problem With Your Order

Because each piece is personalised and made to order, it is exempt from the 14-day change-of-mind right to cancel under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. That never affects your rights when something is wrong: if your print arrives damaged, faulty, misprinted, or different from the preview we emailed you, email admin@shopcienty.com within 30 days of delivery with a photo, and we will send a free replacement or a full refund — return postage on us where a return is needed.

Full detail is on our Delivery & Returns page.
